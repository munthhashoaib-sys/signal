What happens when you hit Generate
1
Fetch the 10-K from SEC EDGAR
Signal calls the US government filing database, finds the company by name, retrieves their most recent annual report, and extracts readable text. No PDF uploads required.
2
Split into searchable chunks
The filing is split into 500-word sections with 100-word overlap. This makes the document searchable by meaning, not just keywords.
3
Find the most relevant sections
Each section is converted into a mathematical representation of its meaning. Signal searches for sections most relevant to a sales conversation — risks, strategic priorities, financial pressures.
4
Chunk Judge scores each section
A second AI evaluates every retrieved section against four criteria: specificity, role relevance, novelty, and product fit. Sections scoring below 7 out of 10 are rejected before anything reaches Claude.
5
Generate 5 openers with Claude
Only approved sections reach Claude. Claude reads the filing evidence alongside your prospect details and writes five cold email openers grounded in specific, verifiable filing disclosures.
6
Opener Judge scores each opener
Every opener is scored against specificity, non-obviousness, conversational tone, and role fit. Scoring 8 or above is approved. Lower scores are flagged with improvements or sent for rewriting.
7
Reviser rewrites rejected openers
If an opener is rejected, the system reads the judge feedback and rewrites only that opener with a targeted correction. It does not regenerate all five — just the one that failed.
